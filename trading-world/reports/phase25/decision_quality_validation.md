## 4. Luck-independence

The same 1,000 decisions (500 at 2x, then 500 at 12x) on two different market paths: on one the account died at decision 856; on the other (no crash in the second half) the account grew +4844%. The scores are identical on both, because they use only the decisions: quality 0.885, ruinous share 50%, episode quality -7.30.

A lucky gambler at a constant 12x on a path that happens to contain no crash ends with realised log growth +5.71 (a large profit). Its scores: ruinous share 100%, episode quality -10.13. The score judges the decision, not the outcome.
